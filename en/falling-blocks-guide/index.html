Falling blocks game tips: how to clear lines and last longer

Keep the stack flat, leave room for the long piece and stay calm as the speed climbs.

A falling blocks game is a classic kind of puzzle where you rotate and place four-square pieces as they drop from the top, filling horizontal lines and clearing them once they are complete. The rule is clear within seconds; the challenge is keeping the board tidy as the pieces speed up. These falling blocks game tips cover the basic rules, why a flat surface matters, how to leave a well for the long piece and how to prepare for faster levels. The last section explains the controls and settings of Falling Blocks in BlockFour.

Falling blocks guide

Falling blocks game rules

The playing field is a vertical well; in BlockFour it is 10 columns wide and 20 rows tall. Every piece is made of four squares, and there are seven shapes: the long straight piece, the square, and the T, S, Z, J and L pieces. Each piece drops on its own; you slide it left and right, rotate it and, if you like, send it down fast.

When a piece lands on the floor or on other blocks, it locks in place and the next piece appears. When a horizontal line is filled from edge to edge, it clears and the blocks above it move down one row. If there is no room for the next piece to appear, the game is over.

Your score depends on how many lines you clear at once. In BlockFour one line is worth 100 points, two lines 300, three lines 500 and four lines 800, all multiplied by your current level. So clearing four lines in one go is worth twice as much as clearing the same four lines one by one.

Keep a flat surface

The secret to lasting a long time is keeping the top of your stack as flat as possible. On a bumpy surface, many pieces do not fit anywhere cleanly and leave holes underneath. A line with a hole in it cannot be completed until everything above it is cleared.

Before you drop a piece, look at the surface and ask: will this leave a hole underneath? If it will, try another rotation or another column. S and Z pieces almost always leave a gap on a flat floor, so try to place them on one-step ledges.

  • Keep the height difference between neighboring columns to one or two squares.
  • Put the square piece on flat areas, and the J and L pieces against walls or into one-step corners.
  • The T piece is very flexible and great for filling small dips.
  • Do not build up the middle. A tall middle makes it hard to move pieces to the edges.

Leave a well for the long piece

The biggest scores come from clearing four lines at once, and only the long straight piece can do that. That is why many players leave a one-column well on one side of the board, usually the far right or far left column, and fill the other nine columns neatly. When four lines are ready, they drop the long piece upright into the well and clear all four.

A well has one risk: if the long piece is late, the stack keeps rising. In BlockFour, pieces come in shuffled groups of seven, with every shape appearing once in each group, so the long piece never stays away for very long. Still, if your stack passes the middle of the board, it is safer to close the well and relieve the pressure with smaller clears.

How to clear lines more reliably: use the preview

Seeing the upcoming pieces is the difference between a good move and a great one. If you think about where the next piece will go while placing the current one, your surface stays much flatter. For example, if the long piece is next, keep your well open; if the square is next, prepare a flat spot two squares wide for it.

The ghost piece is another big help: a faint shadow shows exactly where the piece will land if you drop it. Glancing at the shadow before a fast drop cuts down on pieces dropped into the wrong column.

Speed levels

In this kind of game, the challenge arrives through speed. In BlockFour you go up one level for every 10 lines you clear, and pieces fall a little faster at each level. At the first level a piece drops one row about every 0.85 seconds; from level 12 on it reaches top speed, about one row every 0.1 seconds.

As the speed climbs, your strategy changes. On slow levels there is time to build a well and go for four-line clears; on very fast levels, keeping the stack low and placing pieces in the first good spot matters more. Since points are multiplied by your level, every clear at a high level is worth much more, so staying alive is the best way to score.

Falling Blocks in BlockFour: controls and settings

Falling Blocks in BlockFour is played with on-screen touch buttons. Here are the details specific to the app:

  • There are four buttons: Move left, Move right, Rotate and Fast drop. Hold Left or Right to slide the piece quickly.
  • Rotate turns the piece clockwise. If the piece is next to a wall or other blocks, the game tries a nearby position that fits, so rotating in tight spots usually still works.
  • Fast drop sends the piece down with increasing speed. When it touches down, it waits for a very short moment; if you slide it off an edge during that moment, it keeps falling. Pressing Fast drop when the piece has already landed locks it right away.
  • Next to the board you see the next piece and the two after it, and the board shows a ghost piece where the current piece will land.
  • In Settings, Edit Button Layout lets you drag the buttons wherever you want, choose Small, Medium or Large buttons and use a Right-handed or Left-handed preset.
  • You can pause at any time, and your game saves automatically.
  • The Records screen keeps your best score, your most lines cleared and your highest level. On Google Play Games there are achievements for 10 and 50 lines and for reaching level 5, plus a leaderboard for your best score.
Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do you clear lines in a falling blocks game?

Fill every square of a horizontal line and it clears on its own; the blocks above move down one row. Clearing several lines at once earns more points.

Why is clearing four lines at once so important?

In BlockFour one line is worth 100 points and four lines 800, multiplied by your level. Clearing four at once is worth twice as much as clearing the same lines one by one.

When do I level up, and how fast does it get?

In BlockFour you level up every 10 lines. Pieces get a little faster at each level and reach top speed from level 12 on.

Can I move the on-screen buttons?

Yes. In Settings, Edit Button Layout lets you move the buttons, choose their size and pick a right-handed or left-handed layout.

Can I hold a piece for later?

No, Falling Blocks in BlockFour has no hold slot. Instead you can see the next three pieces in advance and plan around them.

Can I play Falling Blocks offline?

Yes. All four games in BlockFour work without an internet connection. The app is free and contains ads.
