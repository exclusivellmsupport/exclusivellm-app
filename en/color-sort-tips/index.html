Color sort game tips: how to beat every level

Use the empty tube wisely and think a few moves ahead; even the messiest level has a way out.

These color sort game tips start with the goal, which is simple: pour the mixed colored liquids between tubes until each tube holds a single color. In this kind of puzzle, also known as a water sort puzzle, the first levels take a handful of moves; as colors and tubes pile up, it gets easy to walk into a dead end if you do not plan ahead. This guide covers the rules, why the empty tube is your most valuable resource, which tube to start with and what to do when you are stuck. The examples follow Color Sort, one of the four games in BlockFour.

Color sort tips

Color sort game rules

Each tube holds a limited number of liquid layers; in BlockFour that limit is 4. You tap a tube to select it, then tap the tube you want to pour into. A pour only works in two cases: the target tube is empty, or the color on top of the target tube matches the color you are pouring. If the target tube is full, nothing moves.

When you pour, all matching layers on top flow together, but only as many as fit. For example, if a tube has two blue layers on top and you pour it into a tube with blue on top and one free space, only one blue moves and the other stays behind. This detail matters: sometimes you need to free up space in the target first to move exactly the amount you want.

The level is solved when every tube is either empty or filled with four layers of a single color. In BlockFour every color has exactly four layers, so each color ends up filling exactly one tube.

Why the empty tube is gold

The empty tube is the only real flexibility you have. It accepts any color, so you can use it to park a layer for a while, uncover the color underneath, or start collecting a color in a fresh tube. Fill it without thinking and you may have no moves left on the very next turn.

Before you put anything into the empty tube, ask yourself: will I be able to move this layer somewhere else within a few moves, or is this tube about to become that color's permanent home? The best use is to make the empty tube the final home of one color and bring that color's other layers to it one by one.

Most levels start with only one empty tube. In many of them, though, some tubes are not full at the start, and the spare space in those tubes is a small but valuable parking spot too. And whenever you manage to empty a tube completely, you gain a new empty tube and the whole level suddenly opens up.

Which tube should you start with?

  • Look at every tube first and roughly count where the four layers of each color are. Colors on top can move right away; colors buried at the bottom need patience.
  • If a tube is almost done, for example three layers of one color stacked together, make finding and moving the fourth layer a priority. A finished tube is out of the way and frees your mind.
  • Tubes with few layers are good places to start. Emptying a tube that holds only one or two layers gives you a second empty tube.
  • If the same color is on top of two tubes, pour one onto the other to gather that color in one place.
  • If a color is buried at the bottom of several tubes, leave it for last; it will come to the surface as you solve the others.
  • Before each move, think about which tube it opens and which one it covers. If you are covering one color to uncover another, you are probably on the wrong path.

Water sort puzzle strategy for hard levels

On hard levels, it helps to think backwards. Decide which color you can finish first, plan the order in which you need to uncover its layers, and only then make your first move. Random single pours usually fill the empty tube far too early.

Another useful habit is not splitting colors. Moving a single red off a tube that has two reds on top means you will later have to reunite two separate reds. Move matching layers together whenever you can.

What to do when you are stuck

Every level has a solution; if you are stuck, a chain of wrong moves has led you into a dead end. BlockFour gives you two tools for this:

  • Undo: takes back your last pour. There is no limit, so you can step back move by move all the way to the start of the level. No ad is needed.
  • Restart: resets the level to its starting layout. If the board is a mess or you realize your first moves were wrong, this is the quickest fix.
  • Step back a few moves from where you got stuck and look for the first move that filled the empty tube. That is usually where things went wrong.
  • There is no timer. A move counter is shown on screen, but it does not rush you; take as long as you need to think.

How the color sort levels get harder in BlockFour

Color Sort in BlockFour is a path of 50 levels, split into five chapters of ten: Beginner, Explorer, Strategist, Expert and Master. The first five levels have only two colors and three tubes, which makes them ideal for learning the rules. Every five levels add one more color and more tubes. In the last ten levels you play with ten colors and twelve tubes.

Along with the number of colors, the starting mix gets messier; in later levels the layers of one color are scattered across three or four different tubes. A few levels start with two empty tubes, while most have just one. Every level is checked for a solution in the game's tests, so you will never face an unsolvable board.

Small helpers in the game

  • The top of the screen shows your level, your move count and the number of colors in the level.
  • A progress bar shows how many tubes are already sorted.
  • The selected tube lifts slightly, so you always know which tube you are pouring from.
  • If you use a screen reader, the colors in each tube are read out from top to bottom.
  • Your progress saves automatically, and you pick up from the level where you left off.
  • On Google Play Games there are achievements for 1, 10 and 25 solved levels and a leaderboard for the number of levels you have solved.
Questions

Frequently asked questions

What are the rules of a color sort game?

You can only pour a color into an empty tube or onto a tube that has the same color on top. The level ends when every tube is either empty or completely filled with one color.

Is a water sort puzzle the same as a color sort game?

Yes, they are different names for the same type of puzzle. In BlockFour's Color Sort you also pour colored liquids between tubes.

I'm stuck. How do I beat the level?

Undo a few moves and look for the move that filled your empty tube. In BlockFour undo is unlimited and free, and you can also restart the level at any time.

How many color sort levels does BlockFour have?

There are 50 levels. They start with two colors and end with ten colors and twelve tubes, split into five chapters.

Are there levels that cannot be solved?

No. Each of the 50 levels in BlockFour is checked for a solution in the game's tests.

Can I play color sort offline?

Yes. All four games in BlockFour work without an internet connection. The app is free and contains ads.
