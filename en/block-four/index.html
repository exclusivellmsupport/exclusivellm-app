Four puzzle games, one app.

Block Four brings four classic puzzle games together in one app: Block Route, Falling Blocks, Number Merge and Color Sort. Whether you have two minutes or a whole evening, there is always a puzzle that fits your mood. No account, no sign-up, and every game can be played offline.

Block Four

Features

Block Route: 8×8 block puzzle

  • Drag blocks onto the 8×8 board and clear full rows and columns.
  • Clear several lines at once for combos and big bonuses.
  • Three modes: Classic with no time limit, 120 Seconds against the clock, and Daily Route, a new 30-move challenge every day.

Falling Blocks: 10×20

  • Rotate and place falling blocks, complete lines and level up.
  • Blocks fall a little faster at every level; use hard drop to keep up.
  • Move and resize the on-screen buttons and choose a left- or right-handed layout.

Number Merge: 4×4 2048

  • Swipe tiles, merge matching numbers and reach the 2048 tile.
  • 3 free undos in every game.
  • Keep going after 2048 for an even bigger tile.

Color Sort: 50 levels

  • Pour colored liquids between tubes until each tube holds a single color.
  • 50 levels that get harder step by step, with undo and restart.

Features

  • Three visual themes: Pastel, Night and Wood
  • Separate records for every game
  • Auto-save: continue right where you left off
  • Sound effects, vibration and a reduced motion option
  • English, Turkish and Spanish
  • No account or registration needed
Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Block Four free?

Yes, it is free to download on Google Play. The app contains ads; watching an ad to continue after a Classic game over is optional.

Can I play offline?

Yes. All four games work without an internet connection.

Do I need an account?

No. Start playing right away, with no sign-up.

Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish and Spanish.
