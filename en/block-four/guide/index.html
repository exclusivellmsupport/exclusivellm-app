Block Four tips: a guide to all four games

Every game has its own strategy; here is what works best.

Block Four brings four different puzzles together in one app: Block Route, Falling Blocks, Number Merge (2048) and Color Sort. Their rules differ, so the best tactics differ too. In this guide you will find short, practical tips for each game.

Tips

Block Route (8×8)

  • In Classic mode, take your time; there is no timer.
  • In 120 Seconds, don't hunt for the perfect spot: quick, safe placements score more.
  • Daily Route gives you 30 moves; try to make every move clear a line.
  • Clearing several lines at once earns combos and big bonuses.

Falling Blocks (10×20)

  • Keep the surface flat; deep wells and spikes make the game harder.
  • Leave one column empty for long pieces and clear several lines together.
  • Hard drop saves time, but use it only when you are sure of the placement.
  • Move and resize the on-screen buttons to fit your hand, and pick a left- or right-handed layout.

Number Merge (2048)

  • Keep your biggest tile in one corner and never move it out.
  • Swipe mostly in two directions; use the fourth only when you must.
  • Keep the row next to your biggest tile full to protect the corner.
  • You get 3 free undos per game; save them for critical moments.

Color Sort (50 levels)

  • Before your first move, look at every tube and find the easiest color to collect.
  • An empty tube is your most valuable resource; don't fill it right away.
  • Completing one tube with a single color frees up space in the others.
  • If you get stuck, undo or restart the level.
