Exclusive Quiz tips: how to play and score more

Once you know how a round works and where the points come from, your record gets a lot closer.

Our Exclusive Quiz tips start with the basics: you pick a category, answer 15 questions with 20 seconds each and try to find the right one of four answers. But two players with the same number of correct answers can end up with very different scores, because points depend on difficulty, speed and streak. This trivia game guide explains how a round works, how scoring is calculated, what the four game modes do, how to learn from wrong answers and when to use the 50:50 hint.

How a round of Exclusive Quiz works

On the home screen you choose one of ten categories: Geography, Science, History, Nature, Sports, General knowledge, Film, Music, Literature or Technology. You can also choose Mixed to play random questions from every category.

Each round has 15 questions that climb from easy to hard: 5 easy questions first, then 5 medium and finally 5 hard. You have 20 seconds per question and pick one of four answers. When you get a question right, a short explanation appears, so you see why the answer is correct or learn something new. If time runs out, the question counts as wrong.

Questions do not repeat back to back: the game keeps picking new ones until you have seen every question of that difficulty. If you think a question is wrong, you can report it with one tap.

How scoring works in this trivia game

In Exclusive Quiz, every correct answer is worth three parts. The first is difficulty: an easy question is worth 100 points, a medium one 150 and a hard one 200. The second is speed: the faster you answer, the bigger your speed bonus, up to +50, shrinking in line with the time left. The third is your streak: once you have 3 or more correct answers in a row, each correct answer adds a +25 streak bonus.

An example: you are on a streak of four and answer a medium question with 10 seconds left. You get 150 base points, a 25 speed bonus because half your time is left, and a 25 streak bonus, for 200 points in total. A wrong answer or a timeout scores nothing and resets your streak.

Game modes

  • Daily challenge: everyone answers the same 15 questions today. You get one attempt per day, and playing every day grows your daily streak.
  • Sudden death: an endless run of questions that climbs from easy to hard. The first mistake ends the run, and your score is how many you got right in a row. Once per run you can watch a short ad for a second chance.
  • Time attack: answer as many questions as you can in 60 seconds. Every wrong answer costs 5 seconds. Points here come from difficulty and streak only; there is no speed bonus, because speed is already the whole game.
  • My mistakes: a retry round of up to 15 questions you previously answered wrong or ran out of time on, sorted from easy to hard. Get one right and it leaves the list.

Learning from wrong answers

When you answer wrong in Exclusive Quiz, the correct option is not revealed. That is on purpose: if the right answer were shown right away, you would just memorize it and move on. Instead, the question goes into your My mistakes list and you have to find the answer yourself. A short note on screen reminds you of this.

Playing My mistakes regularly is the easiest way to build your general knowledge step by step. If you got a question wrong, think about the topic a little before it comes back; rule out the options one by one and work out why each wrong one is wrong. When you get it right, read the explanation. The question leaves the list, and the mode card on the home screen shows how many questions are still waiting for you.

When to use the 50:50 hint

The 50:50 hint removes two of the three wrong options and leaves you with two choices. You can use up to 4 hints per round and only one per question. The first hint is free; the next three unlock through the ▶ button by watching a short ad, which is completely optional. Using a hint does not lower your score. Once per round you can also add +10 seconds to the clock, again with a short ad.

  • Do not spend your free hint early. Easy questions are worth 100 points and hard ones 200, so the hint does the most good in the last five hard questions.
  • If you are torn between two answers, try ruling one out yourself first. Save the hint for questions where you have no idea at all.
  • Once your streak passes 3, a hint is worth more: a wrong answer also wipes out your streak bonus.
  • In Sudden death a single mistake ends the run, so hints are even more valuable; use one on the first question you are unsure about.
  • Time attack has no 50:50 hint; only the +10 seconds button is available there.
  • Save +10 seconds for questions where you feel you know the answer but need a moment to remember it.

Quick tips for a new record

  • Answer the easy questions fast; that is where speed bonus is easiest to collect.
  • Do not rush the hard questions. Getting a 200-point question right is worth far more than a few seconds of speed bonus.
  • Start with the category you know best and set your record there, then move on to Mixed rounds.
  • Play the Daily challenge every day to keep your daily streak going and see questions from different topics.
  • Track your category records and longest streak on the records screen; the 14 leaderboards and 10 achievements on Google Play Games give you goals too.
Questions

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are in an Exclusive Quiz round?

Each round has 15 questions: 5 easy, 5 medium and 5 hard, with 20 seconds per question. Sudden death and Time attack do not have a fixed number of questions.

How are points calculated?

A correct answer is worth 100 points on easy, 150 on medium and 200 on hard questions. Fast answers earn up to +50 speed bonus, and with a streak of 3 or more each correct answer adds +25.

Does the 50:50 hint lower my score?

No. The hint only removes two wrong options. You can use up to 4 hints per round; the first is free and the next three unlock with an optional short ad.

Why isn't the right answer shown after a wrong answer?

So that you find it yourself instead of memorizing it. The question goes into My mistakes; when you get it right there, it leaves the list and you can read its explanation.

Which languages are the questions in?

English and Turkish. You can switch language in the game with one tap.

Can I play Exclusive Quiz offline?

Yes. The questions work without an internet connection and no account is needed. The app is free and contains ads.
