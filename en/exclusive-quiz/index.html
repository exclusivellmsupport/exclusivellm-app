Features

How to play

  • Pick a category or play a mixed round from all categories.
  • Each round has 15 questions: 5 easy, 5 medium and 5 hard.
  • You have 20 seconds per question; choose the right one of four answers.
  • Harder questions are worth more points, and quick answers score higher.
  • Answer correctly in a row to build your streak.
  • Stuck? Use the 50:50 hint to remove two wrong answers.

Game modes

  • Daily challenge: the same 15 questions for everyone, every day; grow your daily streak.
  • Sudden death: one mistake ends the run; how many can you get right in a row?
  • Time attack: as many right answers as you can in 60 seconds.
  • My mistakes: retry the questions you got wrong and close the gaps.

Categories

  • Geography, Science, History and Nature
  • Sports, General knowledge and Film
  • Music, Literature and Technology
  • Mixed: random questions from every category

Features

  • Questions in English and Turkish; switch language in the game
  • A short explanation for every question you get right
  • No back-to-back repeats
  • 14 leaderboards and 10 achievements with Google Play Games
  • Personal bests for every category and your longest streak
  • Plays offline, no account needed
  • Dark mode and adjustable text size
  • Report any question you think is wrong in one tap

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Exclusive Quiz free?

Yes, it is free to download on Google Play. The app contains ads; watching an ad for an extra 50:50 hint is optional.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The questions work without an internet connection and no account is needed.

Which categories are there?

Geography, Science, History, Nature, Sports, General knowledge, Film, Music, Literature and Technology, plus mixed rounds from all of them.

Which languages does it support?

English and Turkish. You can switch language in the game with one tap.
