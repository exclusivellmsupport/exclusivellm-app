Woodora Classic

Wood block puzzleStrategyOffline

Think. Place. Unwind.

Woodora Classic is a classic block puzzle with a clean wooden look. Place the three wooden blocks you are given on the 8×8 board, clear full rows and columns and improve your best score. Blocks cannot rotate and there is no time limit, so plan where each piece goes without any rush.

How to play

  • Drag the three wooden blocks onto the 8×8 board, or select a block and tap its destination cell.
  • Full rows and columns clear together; blocks cannot rotate.
  • Use all three blocks to receive new ones.
  • Earn 10 points per square and 100 per line; multiple lines and consecutive clears earn bonuses.
  • The game ends when the remaining blocks no longer fit, so plan ahead.

Features

  • Classic wooden design and an 8×8 board
  • A preview that shows how many lines a placement will clear
  • Drag and drop or tap to place
  • Multi-line and streak bonuses
  • Auto-save: continue where you left off
  • Undo your last move once per game with an optional ad
  • Sound and vibration you can switch off
  • English, Turkish, Spanish and Arabic
Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Woodora Classic free?

Yes, it is free to download on Google Play. The app contains ads; watching an ad to undo your last move is optional.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The game plays without internet and needs no account.

Is there a timer?

No. Take your time with every move.

Can blocks be rotated?

No. Blocks cannot rotate, so planning your placements matters.
