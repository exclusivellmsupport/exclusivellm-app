BlockNest

Wood block puzzleRelaxingOffline

A calm block puzzle in warm wooden tones.

BlockNest is a relaxing wood block puzzle with a warm, handcrafted look. Place wooden blocks on the 10×10 board, clear full rows and columns, chain combos and beat your best score. No timer, no pressure: just a calm brain game you can play offline, anytime.

Features

How to play

  • Drag the three wooden blocks from the tray onto the 10×10 grid.
  • Fill a whole row or column to clear it and free up space.
  • Clear several lines in one move for big bonuses.
  • Clear lines move after move to build a combo multiplier up to x5.
  • The game ends when no block fits on the board, so plan ahead.

Features

  • Four wood themes: Classic Walnut, Crimson Mahogany, Nordic Pine and Dark Ebony
  • 18 achievement badges to unlock
  • Undo a move or continue a lost game with an optional ad
  • Gentle flower and butterfly effects when you clear lines
  • Auto-save: continue exactly where you stopped
  • Wooden sound effects, haptic feedback and a reduced motion option
  • English, Turkish, Spanish and Arabic
Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is BlockNest free?

Yes, it is free to download on Google Play. The app contains ads; watching an ad to undo a move or continue a game is optional.

Can I play offline?

Yes. No account and no internet connection are needed.

Is there a timer?

No. There is no rush; take your time with every move.

Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish, Spanish and Arabic.
