BlockNest tips: a guide to higher scores

Keep the roomy 10×10 board open and keep your combo alive.

In BlockNest most of your score comes not from single lines but from back-to-back clears and from clearing several lines in one move. This guide shows how to keep the board tidy, how to carry the combo multiplier up to x5 and how to avoid the mistakes that end a game early.

Tips

Keep the board open

  • Place pieces along the edges and corners first; leave the middle free for big pieces.
  • Avoid single-cell holes: only a 1×1 piece can fill them.
  • Always keep at least one 3×3 area open so a big square never traps you.
  • Keep one row or column clear for long bars (1×5).

Grow your combo

  • Clearing lines move after move raises the combo multiplier up to x5.
  • Instead of closing a line right away, set up a layout that lets you clear again on the next move.
  • Think about all three tray pieces together: which order clears the most lines?
  • Clearing two or three lines in one move scores far more than single lines.

Common mistakes

  • Filling the middle of the board too early.
  • Looking only at the piece in hand and forgetting the other two.
  • Overfilling the board for a combo; once you are stuck, the combo ends too.
  • Rushing: BlockNest has no timer, so think every move through.

Extra help

  • Undo a wrong move with an optional ad.
  • When a game ends, you may get the chance to continue with an optional ad.
  • 18 achievement badges give you small goals to sharpen your play.
  • Choose the wood theme from the four that is easiest on your eyes.
