Our promise

Respect for players is the design.

Many free games interrupt you with an ad every couple of moves. We chose the opposite.

We don't drown you in ads

Ads never interrupt a round; full-screen ads only appear between games, with a strict frequency cap. Rewarded ads are fully optional: watch one if you want a bonus, skip it and lose nothing.
