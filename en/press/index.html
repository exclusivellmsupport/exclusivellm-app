Games

BlockFour

BlockFour brings four classic puzzle games together in one app: Block Route, Falling Blocks, Number Merge and Color Sort. Whether you have two minutes or a whole evening, there is always a puzzle that fits your mood. No account, no sign-up, and every game can be played offline.

  • exclusivellm.app/en/block-four/
  • Google Play: com.firat.blokrota

BlockNest

BlockNest is a relaxing wood block puzzle with a warm, handcrafted look. Place wooden blocks on the 10×10 board, clear full rows and columns, chain combos and beat your best score. No timer, no pressure: just a calm brain game you can play offline, anytime.

  • exclusivellm.app/en/blocknest/
  • Google Play: com.exclusivellm.blocknest

Woodora Classic

Woodora Classic is a classic block puzzle with a clean wooden look. Place the three wooden blocks you are given on the 8×8 board, clear full rows and columns and improve your best score. Blocks cannot rotate and there is no time limit, so plan where each piece goes without any rush.

  • exclusivellm.app/en/woodora-classic/
  • Google Play: com.exclusivellm.woodoraclassic

Exclusive Quiz

Exclusive Quiz is a fast, fun trivia game that puts your general knowledge to the test. From geography and history to film and technology, questions in 10 categories are waiting for you. Every round has 15 questions that climb from easy to hard, with 20 seconds for each. Find the right answer, keep your streak alive and beat your record.

  • exclusivellm.app/en/exclusive-quiz/
  • Google Play: com.exclusivellm.quiz
