Offline games to play on an airplane: your pre-flight checklist

5 checks before you fly

  • Download and update: install your games at home or at the hotel on Wi-Fi, and apply any pending Google Play updates. A big download over slow airport Wi-Fi takes ages and may not finish.
  • Open each game once: launch every game at least once while online and play a round. Language choices, permission windows or a short intro can appear the first time; get them out of the way on the ground.
  • Test in airplane mode: switch the phone to airplane mode, close the app completely, reopen it and start a game. It is the only reliable way to know a game truly works offline.
  • Battery and brightness: charge fully, turn on battery saver and lower brightness to suit the cabin lights. Closing apps left open in the background helps too.
  • Headphones and sound: playing out loud in a cabin bothers people around you. Pack headphones or mute the game in its settings, and turn off vibration so your neighbor is not disturbed.

Games for short flights (1 to 2 hours)

On a short flight, the time between boarding, takeoff and landing is short and announcements come often. Pick games that open fast, finish in a few minutes and are not lost when you put them down halfway.

  • Woodora Classic: short, focused games on an 8×8 board. Put it down when the landing announcement comes; the game saves automatically and continues where you left off.
  • Exclusive Quiz: a round is 15 questions, and Time attack lasts at most 60 seconds (each wrong answer costs 5), a good fit for a short wait.
  • Color Sort in BlockFour: each level is a quick puzzle, and you work through 50 levels step by step.

Games for long flights with no Wi-Fi (3 hours or more)

On a long flight one game can get old; rotating between two or three games with different pace makes the time pass better. Battery matters more too, so short breaks from the screen and low brightness pay off.

  • BlockNest: games on the 10×10 board tend to run long and there is no timer, so you can go for a high score without rushing. The Daily Challenge needs no connection either.
  • BlockFour: four games in one. Switch between Block Route, Falling Blocks, Number Merge (2048) and Color Sort to change the pace.
  • Exclusive Quiz: work through the categories, push your streak in Sudden death, and retry the questions you missed in My mistakes.

What works offline in our games

The four games below are made by us and are free on Google Play; they all contain ads. The games themselves need no internet; ad-unlocked extras and Google Play Games features will most likely be unavailable without a connection. Here is what to expect, game by game:

  • BlockNest: the classic game, the Daily Challenge, in-game achievement badges, records and all settings work without a connection. The ad-based undo and continue may not be available in the air.
  • Woodora Classic: the whole game plays offline and saves automatically. The ad-based undo of your last move may not work without a connection.
  • BlockFour: all four games play without a connection. The 3 free undos per game in Number Merge and the undo and restart in Color Sort need no ad, so they work on the plane. Continuing a Classic game with an ad needs a connection.
  • Exclusive Quiz: the questions come inside the app, so categories, Daily challenge, Sudden death, Time attack and My mistakes all play offline. The first 50:50 hint each round is free; extra hints and +10 seconds are unlocked with an ad, so they may not appear in the air.
  • In all four games, Google Play Games leaderboards and achievements need internet; you will have to wait until you are back online to see scores on the leaderboard.

Small tips for playing in the air

  • Put the game down without worry during takeoff and landing announcements; our games keep your progress on the phone.
  • In Exclusive Quiz the question timer stops when you leave the app, so locking the phone during an announcement does not waste your time.
  • Low screen brightness saves battery and keeps the light out of your neighbor's eyes in a dark cabin.
  • If you use wireless headphones, you can turn Bluetooth back on while in airplane mode; follow the airline's and the crew's rules.
  • After your pre-flight test, check the update list in Google Play; an automatic update that starts right before takeoff may not finish.
Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you play games in airplane mode?

Yes, games that do not need internet play fine in airplane mode. Airplane mode only switches off connections; the phone and your installed apps keep working.

Which of your games work offline?

Every game mode in BlockNest, Woodora Classic, BlockFour and Exclusive Quiz plays without a connection. Only ad-unlocked extras and Google Play Games leaderboards need a connection.

Will I see ads on the plane?

New ads usually cannot load without a connection, though an ad loaded earlier may still appear.

Do I need the in-flight Wi-Fi?

No. In-flight Wi-Fi is often paid and slow, and our games do not need it to play.

Will I lose my high scores without a connection?

No. Scores and progress are stored on the phone and are saved without internet too.

What is the one check to do before boarding?

Switch to airplane mode, close the game and open it again. If it opens and you can play a round, it will work on the plane.
